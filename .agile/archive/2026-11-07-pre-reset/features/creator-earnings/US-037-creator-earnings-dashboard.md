# US-037: Creator Earnings Dashboard

**Status:** backlog
**Priority:** high
**Feature:** creator-earnings

## Description

As an approved creator, I want to see my earnings and the purchases behind them, so that I understand what I have earned and what I can withdraw.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `/studio/earnings` shows estimated/pending, available, paid and on-hold amounts separately, clearly labeled as estimates where applicable
- [ ] The transaction list shows each purchase with gross, commission and creator share
- [ ] A creator sees only their own earnings
- [ ] Moving earnings from pending to available follows a configurable policy, not a hardcoded rule
