# Feature: Creator Content Studio

**Slug:** creator-content
**Handoff phase:** Phase 3 — Content, media and entitlement

## Overview

Approved creators create posts, upload images and video to private storage, choose free or premium, set a per-post Koin price (permanent unlock, 5–1,000 Koin per Monetization Spec v1), pick a safe blurred cover, publish, and archive/restore.

## Stories

- US-015: Create and Edit Draft Posts (high)
- US-016: Upload Images to Private Storage (high)
- US-017: Upload Video to Posts (medium)
- US-018: Set Premium Post Price & Duration (high) — superseded in part by US-046
- US-019: Publish a Post (high) — "hide" becomes archive in US-047
- US-046: Permanent Unlock Offer (5–1,000 Koin) (high)
- US-047: Archive & Restore a Post (high)
- US-048: Safe Cover & Blurred Preview for Premium Posts (high)
- US-049: Upload Limits & Stale Upload Cleanup (medium)
