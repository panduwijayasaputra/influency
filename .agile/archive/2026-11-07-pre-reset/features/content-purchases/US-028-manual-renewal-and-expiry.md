# US-028: Manual Renewal & Expiry

**Status:** backlog
**Priority:** high
**Feature:** content-purchases

## Description

As a subscriber, I want my subscription to end when its period is over unless I renew it myself, so that I am never charged without my action.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Koin are never deducted automatically when a subscription expires
- [ ] After the end date, premium posts covered only by the subscription are locked again
- [ ] Renewing while still active extends the end date from the current end date (no prepaid time lost)
- [ ] Renewing after expiry starts a new period from now
