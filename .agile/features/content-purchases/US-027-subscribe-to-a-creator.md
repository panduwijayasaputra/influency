# US-027: Subscribe to a Creator

**Status:** backlog
**Priority:** high
**Feature:** content-purchases

## Description

As a logged-in user, I want to subscribe to a creator with Koin, so that I can view all their current and future premium posts during the subscription.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Subscribing debits the plan price once and grants access to all of that creator's premium posts until the end date
- [ ] The subscription covers only that creator; other creators stay locked
- [ ] Insufficient balance, duplicate requests and concurrent requests behave the same safe way as post unlocks
- [ ] A creator cannot be charged for subscribing to their own page
- [ ] A user can hold subscriptions to several creators at once
