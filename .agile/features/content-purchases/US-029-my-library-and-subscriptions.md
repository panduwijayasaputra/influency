# US-029: My Library & Subscriptions

**Status:** backlog
**Priority:** medium
**Feature:** content-purchases

## Description

As a logged-in user, I want to see the posts I unlocked and the creators I subscribe to, so that I know what I have access to and when it expires.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `/library` lists unlocked posts with their expiry dates, marking expired ones
- [ ] `/subscriptions` lists subscriptions with creator, status and end date, and a renew action
- [ ] Dates are shown in Asia/Jakarta time
- [ ] Users only see their own purchases
